# Brag Plan: Kural

## What is this app?
An autonomous Tamil–English ("Tanglish") AI voice agent for Tamil Nadu: a citizen calls one number, speaks a civic complaint naturally, and Kural identifies the issue, resolves the exact owning government body, computes any bill over-charge, files an SLA-tracked ticket, and tracks it on a live 37-district dashboard.

## The angle
"One call, the right door." The premise is the quiet miracle of routing: a messy spoken Tanglish sentence goes in; a correctly-routed, SLA-tracked, over-charge-flagged ticket comes out — across agencies that today are five separate silos. Show the product *doing* that, not describing it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The copper **கு** glyph blooms on warm beige; the wordmark **Kural** lands with the line **"One voice call. The right department."** / Tamil **"ஒரே அழைப்பு. சரியான துறை."** Confident, premium, still.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A real caller line types out in Tanglish: *"200 units use pannen, aana bill ₹2000 vandhuchu"* while a copper listen-pulse breathes (the product in use).
- The brain resolves, as chips arrive one by one: **Electricity → TNPDCL → ⚡ ₹1,765 over-charge → CGRF escalation** (cross-agency routing + billing anomaly — the novelty).
- A confidence/stat row: **31 sectors · 37 districts · Tanglish voice · self-evaluating** (KuralEval).

## Outro / punchline
Market tailwind line — **"Tamil Nadu is investing ₹398 cr in AI governance."** — then the கு logo + **"Kural · குரல் — the people's voice."**

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result: **speak a Tanglish complaint → Kural routes + computes → SLA ticket filed, over-charge flagged.** Scenes 2–3 are the centerpiece and show this flow.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: an assured, premium product film pitched like an expert founder — restraint, confidence, no gimmicks.
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, soft crossfades; motion is calm and deliberate; every line gets time to read.

## Duration: ~20 seconds

## Share copy (draft)
A Tamil citizen just speaks — "200 units, but ₹2000 bill?" — and Kural routes it to TNPDCL, flags the ₹1,765 over-charge, and files an SLA ticket. One voice call, the right department. குரல்.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed, low and confident; final fade under the logo.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled), low volume (~0.5), fade-out over last ~1.5s.
- Music treatment: steady bed; let one strong cue near the routing payoff (~11-12s) coincide with the ⚡ over-charge chip.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-...music-cues.json` — target 1 strong cue for the over-charge reveal; snap the 4 routing chips to consecutive beats (hold the full set after).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the listen-pulse glow may breathe with music RMS; nothing waveform-y.
- SFX posture: sparse, polished — a soft key tick under the typed line, one card tick per routing chip, one low confirm cue on the ticket-filed payoff.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed caller line (key ticks), routing chips (card ticks, beat-gridded), over-charge chip (strong cue), ticket payoff (confirm).
- Restraint rule: no busy percussion, no strobing, no stingers on every cut — confidence through quiet.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / brand — 4.5s
Warm beige grain. The copper **கு** glyph blooms (scale+fade) center; **Kural** wordmark slides up; line **"One voice call. The right department."** and Tamil **"ஒரே அழைப்பு. சரியான துறை."** settle and hold.
Sequential/interaction: none (single confident reveal).
Audio intent: bed enters warm and low.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: warm bed in.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The call (product in use) — 5s
A phone/call panel: caller chip "Senthil · Kuniyamuthur, Coimbatore"; the Tanglish line **"200 units use pannen, aana bill ₹2000 vandhuchu"** types out; a copper listen-pulse breathes beside "Kural listening…".
Sequential/interaction: yes — the line types character-by-character; pulse breathes.
Audio intent: intimate, attentive.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks under the typing.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The brain routes (payoff) — 6s
Glass panel titled "Routing…". Four chips arrive one by one: **Electricity**, **TNPDCL (TANGEDCO)**, **⚡ ₹1,765 over-charge**, **CGRF escalation**. A small Coimbatore pin drops on a copper TN silhouette. Then a green **"SLA ticket filed · SMS sent"** bar confirms.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 chips beat-gridded (hold the set); over-charge chip on the strong cue; confirm bar last.
Audio intent: builds to a clean payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: card tick per chip; strong cue on ⚡ over-charge; low confirm on the ticket bar.
Music: bed lifts into the cue.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Proof + tailwind + outro — 4.5s
Stat row: **31 sectors · 37 districts · Tanglish voice · self-evaluating**. Then the context line **"Tamil Nadu is investing ₹398 cr in AI governance."** The கு logo returns with **"Kural · குரல் — the people's voice."** Bed fades out.
Sequential/interaction: stats settle together, then the tailwind line, then logo.
Audio intent: resolve and settle.
Audio-coupled idea: none; let the bed fade.
Music: fade-out under logo.
Transition mood: soft fade to hold.
