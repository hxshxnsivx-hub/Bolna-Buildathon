# Kural — Demo + Pitch Video Script (HeyGen-ready)

**Length:** ~2.5–3 min. **Flow:** Hook → **Live call demo** → **Dashboard demo** → How it works + why it's different → **Future** → Close.

**How to read this:**
- 🎙️ **SAY** = narration — paste this into HeyGen as the presenter's lines.
- 🎬 **SHOW** = what's on screen at that moment (your brag film, the call, the dashboard).
- 🔊 **AUDIO** = the real call recording.

**How to assemble in HeyGen:** make each section a *scene*. Presenter (avatar or your cloned voice) speaks the 🎙️ lines. For the demo scenes, drop in your **real call recording** and your **dashboard screen-recording** as the visuals and let the narration sit over them. Turn on auto-captions. Use a Tamil voice for the call portion.

---

## 1 · HOOK + PITCH  (~0:00–0:25)

🎬 **SHOW:** Open on the first 3 seconds of your brag film (the கு logo + "Kural"), then cut to the presenter.

🎙️ **SAY:**
> "In Tamil Nadu, when something breaks — no water, a wrong power bill, a dark streetlight — complaining is hard. You have to know *which* of five government offices owns the problem, find the right number, and use an app in English. So most people just don't. And it never gets fixed.
>
> Kural fixes that — with one phone call. Let me show you a real one."

---

## 2 · THE DEMONSTRATION  (the heart of the video)

### 2a · The live call — the voice agent working  (~0:25–1:05)

🎬 **SHOW:** A phone-call screen or waveform: "Incoming call → Kural." Play your **real recorded call**. Put bilingual captions on screen (Tanglish + a small English gloss).

🔊 **AUDIO (the real call — this is the script it follows):**
> **Kural:** *"Vanakkam, idhu Kural helpline. Unga prachanaya sollunga."*
> *(Hello, this is the Kural helpline. Tell me your problem.)*
>
> **Caller (Senthil):** *"Kuniyamuthur, Coimbatore. Naan 200 units thaan use pannen, aana bill rendaayiram vandhuchu."*
> *(Kuniyamuthur, Coimbatore. I only used 200 units, but the bill came to ₹2,000.)*
>
> **Kural:** *"Puriyudhu, Senthil. Idhu over-charge maadhiri theriyudhu. Naan idha TNPDCL-ku anuppi, over-charge-a flag panren. Ticket number SMS-la varum. Sari-aa?"*
> *(Understood, Senthil. This looks like an over-charge. I'll send it to TNPDCL and flag the over-charge. Your ticket number will come by SMS. Okay?)*
>
> **Caller:** *"Sari, nandri."* *(Okay, thank you.)*
>
> **Kural:** *"Ticket KRL-2026-00001 file aayiduchu. CGRF-ku escalate aagum. Nandri."*
> *(Ticket KRL-2026-00001 has been filed. It will escalate to CGRF. Thank you.)*

🎙️ **SAY (presenter, right after the call):**
> "No app. No English. He just *spoke* — and Kural understood him, picked the exact office, and caught that he'd been overcharged."

### 2b · The dashboard — see it land, live  (~1:05–1:35)

🎬 **SHOW:** Screen-record the live dashboard. Slowly move across: the new ticket **KRL-2026-00001** with the **"₹1,765 over"** flag → **routed to TNPDCL** → the **Anna Nagar hotspot (3× reporters)** → the **KuralEval score** → the routing-load chart.

🎙️ **SAY:**
> "And here it is — live. That call is now a tracked ticket, routed to the exact office, with a deadline. It worked out he was overcharged by ₹1,765 and flagged it for appeal. When several people in the same area report the same thing, Kural groups them into a hotspot. And over here — it even grades its own call quality, in Tamil."

## 3 · HOW IT WORKS + WHY IT'S DIFFERENT  (~1:35–2:15)

🎬 **SHOW:** Presenter, with a simple 1-line flow behind them: *voice → understand → route → file → track → self-check.*

🎙️ **SAY:**
> "Here's how it works. You call one number. The voice agent — built on Bolna and Plivo — listens in Tamil, English, or a mix. Then a deterministic 'brain' does the hard part: it identifies the issue, finds the exact office that owns it, computes any over-charge, files a ticket with a deadline, and texts you the number.
>
> Now — voice bots exist. What makes Kural different is three things. **One**, it routes to the *exact owning office*, not just a department. **Two**, it checks its own call quality *in Tamil* — something the English-first tools don't do. **Three**, it works *today*, while the state's own voicebot is still an announcement. Tamil Nadu is investing ₹398 crore in exactly this — Kural shows what it should look like."

---

## 4 · THE FUTURE  (~2:15–2:40)

🎬 **SHOW:** Presenter, or clean text cards for each point.

🎙️ **SAY:**
> "Where this goes next — four things.
> **One** — KuralEval becomes a standalone quality layer that *any* Tamil voice agent can plug into.
> **Two** — a missed-call callback, so even a basic phone with no balance can reach it.
> **Three** — Kural calls you *back* when your issue is resolved, and connects straight into the state's official CM Helpline system.
> **Four** — those hotspots become an early-warning map that alerts departments *before* complaints pile up."
